# ChatGPT humanise

Rewrite the text so it reads like a careful human grader wrote it by hand, not an AI.

Keep exactly:
- Every fact, finding, slide number, figure and score.
- Every phrase quoted from the deck or the task files. Text inside quotation marks stays word for word.
- The labels at the start of lines, such as "Completeness:" or "Rank 1:", and the line breaks between them.
- The meaning and the verdict. Do not add findings, drop findings, soften criticism or make it harsher.
- Roughly the same length.

Change:
- Stock formulas and slogan cadence: "not only X but also Y", "X, not Y" reveals, triads like "clear, concise and compelling".
- Em dashes, and colons or semicolons used for drama. Use plain sentences instead.
- AI-flavoured words: delve, robust, pivotal, seamless, comprehensive, underscore, foster, showcase, notably, furthermore, moreover, overall, ensures, highlights the importance of.
- Inflated adjectives and vague praise. Say what the slide actually shows.
- Throat-clearing openers and closing lines that only restate what was said.

Style:
- Plain, simple English with short declarative sentences. British spelling.
- Start points from the evidence, for example "Slide 4 shows that...", then say the effect.
- Use simple joining words such as "However,", "Also,", "Similarly,", "which", "although".
- Vary sentence length a little so it does not read as a template.
- No markdown, no bold, no bullet symbols unless the original had them.

Return only the rewritten text, with nothing before or after it.
